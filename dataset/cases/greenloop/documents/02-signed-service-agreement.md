# Food waste collection agreement — GL-AG-JTS-K7M

Executed on 2023-05-15 in Northbridge.

**Provider:** Mosswheel Services Ltd, Company record TEST-ORG-MWS-Q8T, 28 Bracken Wharf, Northbridge.
Represented by Daniel Mercer, managing director. Correspondence: contracts@mosswheel.example.

**Customer:** Juniper Table Services Ltd, Company record TEST-ORG-JTS-P9X, 17 Cinder Lane, Northbridge.
Represented by Grace Miller, managing director. The customer trades as Copper Kettle.

The customer operates workplace dining rooms and neighbourhood coffee counters.
The provider will remove separated kitchen scraps and coffee grounds, then arrange their delivery to a receiving treatment facility.
The service starts on 2023-05-24. The customer remains responsible for the contents of each container until collection is recorded.

## Meaning of a visit
A visit is a vehicle or cycle stopping at an agreed site to remove the accepted containers.
Exchanging several containers at one stop does not create several visits. A second stop is recorded separately.
The net weight is the measured contents after the container tare has been deducted.
An estimate used for planning is to be identified as an estimate in the monthly statement.

## Schedule and equipment
Regular sites receive a weekly service; higher-volume kitchens may receive additional weekly visits in the accepted route schedule.
Core streets are served by cargo cycle where access allows. The outer route uses an electric van.
The provider lends lidded containers, supplies sorting labels and demonstrates safe loading to the site supervisor.
The customer keeps lids closed and leaves a clear path between the storage point and the pickup entrance.
The proposed capacities are 48 L for coffee counters, 88 L for shared dining rooms and 144 L for production kitchens.

## Service responsibilities
The provider will keep a record of the day, collection location, containers removed and available weight for each recorded stop.
Monthly invoices will identify the service period. A monthly waste statement and an annual customer review will accompany the account service.
When a load is rejected, the provider will identify the reason and the action requested from the customer.
Treatment may be arranged through another operator; the provider remains the customer's contact for the contracted service.

The customer will separate food residues from packaging, cleaning liquids and other unsuitable materials.
The site supervisor will notify the provider if a locked gate, construction work or a kitchen closure prevents access.
Missed access is recorded separately from a completed removal. An empty container return can be recorded with a zero reading.
If the scale fails, the visit record will retain its identifier and show that a weight is unavailable.

## Charges and invoicing
The ordinary core-route charge is GBP 14.83 for each completed visit, excluding tax.
Outer-route prices require an accepted site quotation. Treatment is billed separately at GBP 177.29 per metric tonne.
Opening a newly accepted site incurs a single setup charge of GBP 67.19.
The monthly invoice covers the previous month's service. Tax is shown separately using the account's 16 percent service tax.

**Payment is due 30 days after the invoice issue date.** A shorter term printed on a later invoice changes this agreement only if both parties sign an amendment.
Late balances carry simple interest at **4.6 percent per year** from the day after the agreed due date.
This agreement sets **no fixed recovery fee**. A claim for another collection expense must identify that expense and its basis.

## Review of prices
The parties may review routes and charges at each anniversary, using the cost of serving the accepted sites.
**A revised price takes effect only through a written change signed by both parties.**
An internal rate sheet or a changed invoice template does not itself amend this agreement.
Until such a change is signed, the last agreed charge remains the contractual schedule.

## Term, interruptions and ending service
The initial term runs for one year from commencement and renews annually.
Either party may prevent renewal by delivering written notice at least eight weeks before the renewal date.
The customer may leave early with six weeks of written notice and a charge equal to two months of the preceding average service fees.
If a serious breach remains uncorrected for twenty-one days after a written request, the other party may end the service by notice.
An extended disruption beyond either party's reasonable control must be reported promptly, with the affected routes identified.
After seventy-five days of continuing disruption, either party may end the affected service without an early-exit charge.

At the end of service the parties will arrange the return of equipment, reconcile the last invoices and identify any outstanding records.
A system status showing that a site is inactive does not replace a termination notice.

## Insurance and responsibility
Each party will maintain insurance appropriate to its activities and provide confirmation on request.
The provider is responsible for damage caused by its failure to carry out the agreed collection with reasonable care.
The customer is responsible for safe access and accurate information about the material presented for removal.
Neither party promises compensation for the other's lost business opportunities under this agreement.

## Information and disagreements
Commercial records and personal contact details shared for this service are restricted to people who need them to perform the agreement.
This restriction lasts for seven years after the service ends. It does not cover information already public without a breach or independently developed information.
Required disclosure must be limited to what is required, with advance notice to the other party when permitted.
The parties will first refer a disagreement to their named managers, then agree an independent mediator if needed.
