## Measurement and estimation method
Weighing is frequently omitted on operating rounds. Circuit A serves Mill Road, Roast Kitchen and Hearth counter;
the driver does not use the scale on the runs listed in the register. Tickets still record the date, site and containers.
A not_weighed ticket has no raw_kg. It is a completed collection, not a zero or a cancelled visit.

For each missing net reading, use the site's arithmetic mean of net readings in the same service month.
If that site has no net reading in the month, use its readings in the immediately previous month.
Round the mean to 0.001 kg, then apply that value to each unweighed visit. Do not use another site's readings.
The raw ticket column stays blank; the calculated quantity appears only in estimated_kg.
A printed zero remains a recorded zero and is included in a mean if that site has one.
A negative tare is a scale reading, not a net material mass; the same mean rule supplies its net estimate.
The May tare records fall outside this customer reporting window.

In the four-month update, 63 of 230 tickets have no weighing.
The site-by-month table on page 5 totals **4334.497 kg weighed** and **2736.388 kg estimated**,
giving **7070.885 kg**; the estimated share of this update is **38.70 percent**.
February and April have no readings at Mill Road, Roast Kitchen and Hearth counter; their means come from January and March respectively.
The 2024 receiving-load and invoice schedule is carried in weighed_kg as **13334.000 kg**; it has no per-visit split in this edition.
December retains the account's actual-recovered description. That earlier schedule supplies the prior-year column basis.
Across the report period, the schedule presents **17668.497 kg in weighed_kg** and **2736.388 kg in estimated_kg**:
**20404.885 kg in total**, with **13.41 percent estimated**.
Kilogram columns are added before tonne summaries are rounded to three decimals.
Separate tickets on the same day remain separate rows. The unassigned Hearth label keeps its original fields.
